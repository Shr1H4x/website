About Me

I break things for a living. My own things, on my own machines, up on HackTheBox and TryHackMe. I started writing port scanners and crawlers to figure out how the network actually talks, and it just stuck. These days that's turned into a toolbox I keep adding to.

Right now I'm working through a BSc (Hons) in Cyber Security & Ethical Hacking at Coventry University via Softwarica College in Kathmandu. It's a lot of theory, honestly, so I balance it out by rooting boxes and tearing apart malware samples on the side.

I'm not the guy with all the answers. I'm the guy who'll tinker on something for three days, break it in a new and interesting way, then write up what happened so nobody else hits the same wall.
